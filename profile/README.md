# NowMe.live

**The operating system for small businesses.**

NowMe.live brings the essential tools a business needs into one platform
--- websites, commerce, inventory, CRM, billing, payments, marketing,
employees, and AI-powered automation.

## What We're Building

NowMe.live is designed for small businesses, freelancers, creators, and
agencies that want to run their business without stitching together a
dozen separate tools.

### Core Platform

-   **AI Website Builder** --- Create, customize, and publish
    professional websites without coding.
-   **Website & Page Editor** --- Visual editing with reusable
    components, templates, dynamic data, and AI-assisted design changes.
-   **CRM** --- Manage customers, contacts, interactions, and business
    data.
-   **Inventory** --- Products, stock, warehouses, variants, and
    inventory operations.
-   **Orders & Commerce** --- Manage orders and connect business
    workflows.
-   **POS** --- Support in-person sales and operational workflows.
-   **Billing & Payments** --- Flexible billing and payment
    integrations.
-   **Marketing** --- Connect and manage marketing workflows and
    external platforms.
-   **Employees & Access Control** --- Multi-tenant organizations,
    roles, permissions, and resource-level access.
-   **AI** --- Assist with website creation, content, design, business
    workflows, and automation.
-   **Integrations** --- Connect services such as Shopify, WordPress,
    WhatsApp, Instagram, payment providers, and other platforms.

## Built for Multi-Tenant Businesses

NowMe.live is built around organizations, agencies, users, and
resource-level permissions.

The platform is designed to support:

-   Organizations and agencies
-   Tenant memberships
-   Agency-to-tenant access
-   Roles and permissions
-   Resource-level access control
-   Multiple websites and business entities
-   Usage-based subscriptions
-   Feature and usage limits

## Usage-Based Pricing

Businesses should pay for what they actually use.

The platform supports metered usage across capabilities such as:

-   Websites
-   CRM
-   Inventory
-   POS
-   Orders
-   Employees
-   Storage
-   AI credits
-   Other platform resources

This allows businesses to start small and scale without being forced
into an unnecessarily large plan.

## Security

Security is a core requirement of the platform.

We focus on:

-   Strong authentication
-   Token rotation
-   Tenant isolation
-   Role-based access control
-   Resource-level authorization
-   Secure integrations
-   Usage and subscription enforcement
-   Auditability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please follow the security
reporting instructions provided by the affected repository.
